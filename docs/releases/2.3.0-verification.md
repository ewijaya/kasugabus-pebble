## What was checked

- **Clean build and all automated suites** through `check_release.py --static-only`:
  codec and schema 2 tests, the train reader and connection logic (C output
  compared with the independent Python decoder on 1,100 random queries), the
  phone-to-watch settings parity test, and the stack-depth gate.
- **Emulator runtime on this exact PBW** (`artifacts/releases/2.3.0/runtime.log`
  with its install receipt): launch, navigation (home, board, details, menu,
  Train connection) and update transfer. The transfer served timetable v5
  (schema 2) through the controlled loopback feed and the production phone
  updater: 310 chunks acknowledged by the watch's C code, no PBW reinstall,
  current and future snapshots preserved across stop/start, and the
  interrupted, corrupt, incompatible-manifest and cut-off-download cases left
  stored data intact (`update-harness-5.log`, `update-harness-7.log`).
- **Layout** (six screenshots beside the log): the menu row; the Train screen
  towards Osaka and Kyoto at Large and Extra Large text, where the first screen
  shows station, train, destination and arrival; and the screen shown while the
  timetable has no station times. Earlier builds had the arrival below the fold
  and a stale "bus" time in the subtitle; both were found in screenshots and
  fixed before this build.
- **Physical Pebble Time 2** (owner): this exact PBW was installed through the
  release preparation and the owner approved it. The owner confirmed launch,
  navigation, the sixth menu row and its no-station-times message, the
  v4 dataset on Data status, and the new "Train connection" section and button
  action in the phone settings form. The Train screen with real data was
  checked earlier on a development build that bundled v5, not on this PBW.
- **Schedule and provenance.** Bus data is v4 unchanged except the added minutes
  to each station, read from the same printed column as each departure and
  cross-checked against each pattern's call list for all 1,939 departures. Train
  times come from the JR Odekake and Hankyu pages saved with SHA-256 in
  `data/sources/trains/`; all 2,053 trains were parsed from them.
- **Settings.** The phone and watch parsers agree byte for byte on the new Train
  settings; records saved before 2.3.0 read as the defaults.

## Known gaps and quirks

- No train time has been compared by a person against the railways' own
  timetables, and the Train settings were not saved and re-read from a real
  phone (the form was seen, not submitted).
- `tests/run_emulator_feed.js` has a flaky final cleanup assertion (restoring
  the emulator's original settings): in several runs all seven transfer checks
  passed and it then exited 1. Each sealed capture includes one clean exit.
- The emulator would not install any build until its stored flash image was
  wiped (`pebble wipe`); this matches the retained install-stall diagnosis.
- While testing, the Pebble phone app twice put the store's 2.2.2 back over a
  sideloaded development build of the watch app. This affects only unpublished
  test installs.
- This Linux server's `gh` is 2.4.0 (2022) and lacks `--verify-tag` and
  `--latest`, which `scripts/release.py` passes. After the tool pushed tag
  `v2.3.0` the release step failed; the GitHub release was then created with
  `gh release create` using the same frozen PBW, title and notes file, and
  publication was resumed with the same approved command. The remote tag was
  first confirmed to point at the frozen commit.
- Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
  outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
  outstanding.
- The bus timetable's review is due 2026-11-01 and coverage ends 2026-12-27.
