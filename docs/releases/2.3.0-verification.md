## What was checked

- **Clean build and all automated suites** through `check_release.py --static-only`,
  including the codec/schema 2 tests, the train reader and connection tests
  (C output compared with the independent Python decoder on 1,100 random
  queries), the phone-to-watch settings parity test, and the stack-depth gate.
- **Emulator runtime on this exact PBW** (`artifacts/releases/2.3.0/runtime.log`,
  install receipt alongside): launch, navigation (home, board, details, menu)
  and update transfer. The update transfer served timetable v5 (schema 2)
  through the controlled loopback feed and the production phone updater:
  310 chunks acknowledged by the watch's C code, no PBW reinstall, current and
  future snapshots preserved across stop/start, and the interrupted, corrupt,
  incompatible-manifest and cut-off-download cases left stored data intact.
- **Layout** (five screenshots in `artifacts/releases/2.3.0/`): the menu row and
  the Train screen towards Osaka and Kyoto at Large and Extra Large text. At
  both sizes the first screen shows the station, train, destination and
  arrival. Two earlier layout problems were found this way and fixed before this
  commit (arrival below the fold at Large, then half cut off at Extra Large).
- **Schedule and provenance.** Bus data is v4 unchanged except the added minutes
  to each station, read from the same printed column as each departure and
  cross-checked against each pattern's call list for all 1,939 departures. Train
  times come from the JR Odekake and Hankyu pages saved with SHA-256 in
  `data/sources/trains/`; all 2,053 trains were parsed from them.
- **Settings.** The phone settings form and the watch parser agree byte for byte
  on the new Train settings; records saved before 2.3.0 read as the defaults.

## Known gaps and quirks

- Physical Pebble Time 2: the earlier development build of this code (with v5
  bundled) was run on the owner's watch (launch, Data status v5, Train screen,
  Details line). **This exact PBW has not been installed on the watch**; that
  is the approval step.
- The phone settings form for Train connection has not been opened on a real
  phone, and no train time has been compared against the railways' own
  timetables by a person.
- `tests/run_emulator_feed.js` has a flaky final cleanup assertion (restoring the
  emulator's original settings): in several runs all seven transfer checks passed
  and it then exited 1, and a rerun exited 0. The runs sealed here include one
  clean exit. The failure is in the harness's cleanup, not in a transfer.
- The emulator would not install any build until its stored flash image was
  wiped (`pebble wipe`); this matches the retained install-stall diagnosis.
- Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
  outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
  outstanding.
- The bus timetable's review is due 2026-11-01 and coverage ends 2026-12-27.
