# KasugaBus 2.1.0 — buttons, reminders and favourites on the watch

- **Favourites on the watch.** In a stop list, hold Select to add or remove a
  favourite. Favourites show "Fav" in the list.
- **Up and Down do something.** By default Down shows the next bus and Up
  switches between favourite stops (or the other direction when you have fewer
  than two favourites). Hold Up for the other direction, hold Down for Nearby.
  Back returns to the soonest bus. Change them under Settings → Home buttons
  or in phone settings.
- **Later buses on home.** Up to two later times appear under the destination
  when there is room.
- **App Glance.** The launcher shows the next bus at your stop.
- **Leave-now reminders.** Hold Select on a departure's details for one buzz
  when it is time to leave, based on your walking time and buffer. Add an
  optional heads-up buzz in phone settings. Hold Select again to cancel.
- **Daily commute alarm.** Choose a stop, an earliest time and days; the watch
  buzzes once for the first bus at or after that time on service days.
- **Time profiles.** Open on one stop in the morning and another later.
- **Help** describes the new buttons and holds.

Reminders use scheduled times, not live arrivals, so a delayed bus does not
move the buzz. Timetable content, coverage and the timetable feed are
unchanged.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
outstanding.
