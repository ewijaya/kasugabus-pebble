# KasugaBus 2.2.2 — stop list fix for phone settings

- **New stops appear in phone settings.** Nihon Teien-mae and Handai
  Honbu-mae, added by timetable updates, were missing from the Usual stop and
  favourite lists because settings gave the watch only 2 seconds to send its
  stop list. Settings now wait up to 8 seconds (opening as soon as the list
  arrives) and remember the last complete list on the phone for next time.
- **Saving is never blocked by a slow connection.** If the stop names have not
  arrived, stops the watch already uses show as "Stop #N (name not loaded)"
  and your settings still save, keeping those favourites.

Timetable content is unchanged; the live timetable feed remains v4.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
outstanding.
