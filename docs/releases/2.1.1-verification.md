## Physical diagnosis and result

On the owner's Poco F4 (HyperOS), Nearby showed "Phone unavailable" with
location permission granted. Watch logs over Dev Connection showed the phone
replying within a second with status 5. A diagnostic build reported reason 8:
the companion's fix timestamp failed validation while latitude, longitude and
accuracy were valid. With timestamp normalisation, a test build of the same
Nearby code returned status 0 with all six verified Hankyu points ranked
(accuracy 100 m; Handai East 689 m) on three refreshes, confirmed by watch logs
and a photo of the watch.

## Checks on the exact PBW

- Consolidated automated suites, including timestamp-format and network
  fallback tests, and a clean Emery build passed.
- Emulator launch, navigation and all seven update-transfer scenarios.
- Installed on the owner's watch through Dev Connection before approval.

## Accepted exception and limits

The emulator harness's final preference-restore comparison failed after all
transfer scenarios passed, as in 2.0.1 and 2.1.0; the owner accepted it.
Outdoor accuracy, other phones, battery and the seven-day review were not
observed.
