# KasugaBus 2.2.0 — wrap-around lists and a quicker exit

- **Lists wrap around.** Press Down on the last item to return to the top, or
  Up on the first item to reach the bottom. Holding a button still stops at
  the end, so fast scrolling does not loop.
- **Back goes to the watchface.** Back on the home screen now exits straight
  to the watchface. To keep the previous behaviour, turn on "Back on home
  closes to the app list" in phone settings, or choose it under Settings →
  Home buttons → Back on the watch. Back still returns to the soonest bus
  first if you were browsing later buses.
- **Exit to watchface** is also available as a home button action.
- **日本庭園前 displays fully** on the Japanese stop-name screen.

Timetable content is unchanged; the live timetable feed remains v3.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
outstanding.
