# KasugaBus 2.3.0 — train connection

- **Train connection.** Hold Select → Train connection shows, for the soonest
  bus that reaches JR Ibaraki or Hankyu Ibaraki-shi, the train to catch there
  and when it arrives in Osaka (Umeda for Hankyu) or Kyoto (Kawaramachi for
  Hankyu). The station that gets you there first is shown, with how much later
  the other arrives. Select switches between Osaka and Kyoto; Up and Down
  scroll. Bus Details gets a one-line "Train:" summary, and the new home-button
  action "Train connection" can be bound in Settings → Home buttons (unbound by
  default).
- **Train settings in the phone app.** Open towards Osaka or Kyoto, Kyoto-bound
  destination (Kyoto / Kawaramachi or Takatsuki), always show both stations,
  and the walking minutes from the bus stop to each platform (4 by default).
- **Needs timetable v5.** The bus times to each station arrive with timetable
  v5, which the app downloads on its daily check or from Data status → Select.
  Until then the Train screen says the timetable has no station times yet.
  Older app versions keep the current timetable and are told an app update is
  needed.

Train times are the JR and Hankyu timetables as read on 20 October 2026 and are
due for review on 1 March 2027 (both railways normally change in mid-March); the
screen says when they may be out of date. Times are scheduled, not live.

Also in this release: the All-departures information text moved out of the
app's code into a resource, and the build audit now measures stack depth after
a code layout change once stopped the app from starting on the watch. The app's
code and fixed data use 61,092 of the 65,535 bytes Pebble allows.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
outstanding.
