## Checks

- Consolidated automated suites (including the new extras record, phone
  settings form and source-watch tests) and a clean Emery build passed.
- Emulator on the exact PBW: launch; navigation through home buttons, menu,
  Settings, Home buttons, Help and Nearby; all seven update-transfer scenarios.
- Emulator on the same app source: two leave-now wakeups launched the closed
  app at the scheduled leave time and showed the reminder; the App Glance
  appeared in the launcher; favourite add/remove and later-bus browsing worked.
- Installed on the owner's watch through Dev Connection before publication.

## Store screenshots

The five Emery screenshots were replaced through the Dashboard's Edit Listing
form, as recorded in [the manifest](2.1.0-artwork.json),
[status](../../artifacts/releases/2.1.0/artwork-status.json) and
[read-back](../../artifacts/releases/2.1.0/artwork-readback.json). The banner,
icons and listing fields were unchanged; downloaded images match their sources
within 8 pixels after server PNG optimisation.

## Accepted exception and limits

The emulator harness's final preference-restore comparison failed after all
transfer scenarios passed, as with 2.0.1; the owner accepted this gap. Vibration,
reminders and the glance on the physical watch, the new phone settings sections,
real-phone Nearby, broader GPS/connection/battery behaviour and the seven-day
review were not observed.
