Local buses for Minami-kasugaoka, Ibaraki, Osaka, Japan.

KasugaBus shows the next scheduled Kintetsu and Hankyu buses at eight stops around Minami-kasugaoka: Kasugaoka Koen, Toge, Midorigaoka, Handai Higashiguchi, Handai Igakubu Byoin-mae, Handai Igakubu-mae, Nihon Teien-mae and Handai Honbu-mae. The home screen shows your stop, direction, route, departure time, countdown and the next buses.

- Works offline; timetables update through your phone without reinstalling.
- Favourites, Nearby (approximate distance from your phone's location), departure board and full trip details.
- Leave-now reminders and a daily commute alarm.
- Configurable Up/Down buttons, three text sizes and four themes.
- Times follow the weekday, Saturday and Sunday/holiday timetables in Japan time.

Times are scheduled, not live arrivals. Coverage is local, not all of Osaka.
