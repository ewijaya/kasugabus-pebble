Local buses for Minami-kasugaoka, Ibaraki, Osaka, Japan.

KasugaBus is a neighbourhood bus timetable app for Pebble Time 2, covering six stop groups around Minami-kasugaoka in Ibaraki, Osaka. Coverage is local, not all of Osaka or Japan. Its home combines a clock with your next scheduled departure. Open the board and full details to check the boarding point, operator, route, direction and destination.

Choose Standard, Large or Extra Large text, with Large as the default. Four themes offer vibrant Neon Dark and Neon Light, plus monochrome High Contrast Dark and High Contrast Light. Change these on the watch under Settings or through the phone's app settings. Larger text wraps and scrolls, showing fewer rows when needed.

Browse the bundled Kintetsu and Hankyu timetables offline, select a boarding point and keep your usual favourites. Phone settings let you arrange favourites, choose display preferences and save your own walking allowances. Leave-by times use those allowances only in the explicitly selected Saved origin context.

Know your bus number? Open Stops, then Bus numbers. Choose the operator and number, select your boarding point and direction, and see departures for that bus. Only routes within the six covered stop groups are listed; the filter uses the number when boarding, not a number the bus changes to later.

All departures lets you select several boarding points and see their scheduled departures together, ordered by time with approximate distance breaking ties. Choose Current phone location, Saved home location or manual selection; favourites provide a quick fallback. Every entry identifies its operator, bus number, boarding stop and direction. Points without verified coordinates say Distance unknown and remain selectable.

Save the current phone location as a home reference, or clear it, through an explicit phone settings action. Home coordinates stay private on the phone; only approximate distances and availability states reach the watch. Opening or saving ordinary settings does not request location. Distances are straight-line estimates and never walking times.

Bus times and service dates use Japan time; the large clock follows your watch's local time. Calendar-aware lookup distinguishes weekday, Saturday and Sunday/public-holiday services and shows unconfirmed dates. These are scheduled departures, not live arrivals, bus locations or delay predictions.

Optional Nearby requests one phone location fix and ranks six verified Hankyu boarding poles by approximate straight-line distance. Enable "Use phone location for Nearby" for manual lookups; "Open Nearby on startup" is a separate choice. There is no continuous tracking, and phone location permission is separate from the app setting. Kintetsu boarding points remain available through manual selection. Nearby does not estimate walking time or give roadside guidance.

The initial bundled timetable covers 1 October–27 December 2026. Source review is due 1 November; later service dates remain unconfirmed. Reviewed timetable revisions download through your connected phone without reinstalling the app. Automatic checks run daily when the app opens; Data status also offers a manual check. The bundled timetable remains usable offline. A successful feed check does not certify operator source freshness.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks, outdoor readability, battery observations and seven days of use remain outstanding. Candidate-specific physical observations and automated/emulator results are documented in the source repository.

Version 2.0.0 was published at the owner’s request with some final emulator update/recovery and All departures layout checks deferred. The source verification report records the completed checks and remaining limitations.
