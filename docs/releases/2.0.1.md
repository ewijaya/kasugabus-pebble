# KasugaBus 2.0.1 — larger bus info and hour countdowns

- Bigger bus information on the home screen. Standard now matches the former
  Large, Large matches the former Extra Large, and Extra Large is larger
  still, including a bigger departure time. The clock is more compact, with
  the date beside it, and stays the same at every text size.
- Departures 60 minutes or more away show hours and minutes, such as
  `1 h 10 m`, on the home screen and departure boards.
- If the phone refuses location, Nearby now says to allow the Pebble app's
  location all the time. The KasugaBus Nearby setting cannot grant that
  phone permission.
- Phone settings list Preferences before Usual stop.

Timetable content, coverage and the timetable feed are unchanged. All
departures are scheduled, not live arrival predictions.

Broader physical GPS/permission and connection-loss/update-recovery checks,
outdoor readability, battery observations and the seven-day use review remain
outstanding.
