## Data and physical-use scope

The live [timetable feed](https://ewijaya.github.io/kasugabus-pebble/timetables/manifest.json)
serves reviewed dataset **v2**; the app bundles **v1**. V2 changes only the data
release number. All 1,411 departures, calendars and source dates are unchanged.
Coverage is **1 October–27 December 2026**, with source review due
**1 November 2026**. A successful feed check does not establish source freshness.

The owner's setup is a Time 2 on firmware **4.38.4**, Poco F4 with
**Android 14 (UKQ1.231207.002)** and Pebble companion **1.14.0.1**.
Final-candidate installation and explicit physical approval are recorded.
Earlier navigation/icon observations and the owner's successful physical v2
update remain bound to their original development PBWs in the
[full verification history](../VERIFICATION.md), as do earlier native
controlled future-revision and interrupted-transfer experiments.

Broader physical GPS/permission, Clay persistence, connection-loss, future
activation and update-recovery checks remain outstanding, along with outdoor
readability, battery observations and seven days of actual use. Publication
does not assert these checks passed. The owner accepted these limitations for
this initial release; they are also stated in the public description and notes.
