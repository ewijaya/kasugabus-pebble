HOME
Next scheduled bus from your stop, with its route, time, countdown and later buses.
DOWN: next bus  UP: switch favourite stop
Hold UP: other direction  Hold DOWN: Nearby
BACK: soonest bus again, then the watchface
SELECT: departure board
Hold SELECT: menu
Other direction applies where a stop has more than one boarding direction. Change these in Settings > Home buttons.

BOARD
UP/DOWN: choose a bus
SELECT: full details
Hold SELECT: refresh

LISTS
Press DOWN on the last item to return to the top (UP wraps too). Holding a button stops at the end.

DETAILS
Hold SELECT: buzz once when it is time to leave (walking time + buffer). Hold again to cancel.

FAVOURITES
In a stop list, hold SELECT to add or remove a favourite.

MENU
Stops: favourites, Nearby, stop list, bus numbers and All departures
Trip context: walking time from your saved origin
Data status: timetable dates and update check
Settings: text size, colour theme and more

PHONE
In the Pebble app, open KasugaBus settings for favourites, buttons, commute alarm, time profiles, walking times, location and display.

Times are scheduled (JST), not live arrivals.
